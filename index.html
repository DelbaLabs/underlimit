About UnderLimit

UnderLimit is a simple utility for reducing photos and PDFs to a maximum file size.

Photo and PDF processing is performed locally on the device. UnderLimit is designed with privacy in mind and does not require an account to compress files.

UnderLimit is developed by Delba Labs.
